## Get Started with NodeLoc in Three Steps

Quickly complete registration, join discussions, and learn the community's core features.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/home-logged-in.png" alt="NodeLoc homepage" />
</Frame>

### Step 1: Sign Up and Log In

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="user-plus" title="Create an account">
    1. Open the [NodeLoc homepage](https://www.nodeloc.com).
    2. Click the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner.
    3. Enter a username, email, and password to complete registration.

    <Tip>Use a real email address so you can recover your password and receive notifications. You can also log in directly with a Google, GitHub, X, or Telegram account.</Tip>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="log-in" title="Log in to the forum">
    1. Click the "Log In" button in the top-right corner.
    2. Enter the email/username and password you registered with.
    3. Once logged in, you can set your avatar and signature in [Preferences](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/preferences).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 2: Join the Community

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="messages-square" title="Post and reply">
    - On the homepage, pick a node (section) that interests you and click "New Topic" to create a post.
    - Read other users' posts and click "Reply" to join the discussion.
    - Use the editor toolbar to add images, videos, emoji, and formatted text.
    - See [Posting & Replying](/en/getting-started/posting) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="calendar-check" title="Daily check-in for Energy">
    - After logging in, there's a "Daily Check-in" button in the top-right corner of the page.
    - Click it once a day to claim an Energy reward.
    - See [Daily Check-in](/en/energy/checkin) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="trophy" title="Energy and trust levels">
    - You earn Energy by posting, replying, receiving likes, and more.
    - As your activity grows, your trust level increases, unlocking more permissions such as chat, posting links, and creating nodes.
    - Check your Energy: [Profile → Energy](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/points-history).
    - See [Energy & Trust Levels](/en/level) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="bell" title="Notifications and private messages">
    - Click the bell icon in the top-right corner to view new notifications.
    - Once you reach Silver Member (TL1), you can initiate private messages.
    - You can adjust notification preferences in settings, and also link [Telegram notifications](/en/common/telegram).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Step 3: Community Rules and Safety

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="gavel" title="Follow community rules">
    - Read the [Community FAQ](https://www.nodeloc.com/faq) and [Terms of Service](https://www.nodeloc.com/tos) to understand posting guidelines.
    - Respect others and avoid posting content that violates the rules.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="shield-check" title="Account security">
    - Use a strong password. You can change your password and add a Passkey under Preferences → Security.
    - If you notice account issues or abusive behavior, contact an administrator promptly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
