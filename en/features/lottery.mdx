The lottery is one of the most popular community activities on NodeLoc, commonly found in the [lottery node](https://www.nodeloc.com/c/events/lottery/12). The host sets up the prizes, participants buy tickets with Energy, and the draw happens automatically when the time comes.

## Joining a Lottery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reply to the topic">
    You must reply to the topic before joining its lottery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy tickets">
    Click the "Join Lottery" button on the lottery card, choose the number of tickets, and confirm. Each ticket costs **1 Energy**. You can also choose "Random" to buy a random number of tickets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the draw">
    The draw happens automatically once the draw time arrives and the minimum participant count is met. Winners are informed via notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/lottery-widget.png" alt="Lottery card in a topic" />
</Frame>

### Participation Rules

- Each ticket costs 1 Energy; the more you buy, the higher your chance of winning.
- **Each user can win at most once**: no matter how many tickets you buy, a user can only win one prize per lottery.
- Participants must reach the minimum trust level set for the lottery (Silver member TL1 or above by default).
- You cannot join a lottery you created yourself.
- Each lottery has a "maximum tickets per user" limit, set by the host.

## Hosting a Lottery

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new topic">
    Create a topic in a suitable node (usually the lottery node). After writing the content, click the "Lottery" button at the bottom of the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the lottery">
    Fill in the settings window:
    - **Lottery title**
    - **Minimum/maximum participants**: if the minimum is not met, the lottery is canceled (no draw)
    - **Maximum tickets per user**
    - **Minimum level**: the minimum trust level required of participants
    - **Draw date**: at most 30 days from creation
    - **Prizes**: you can set multiple tiers (first prize, second prize, etc.), each with its own name, quantity, and prize content
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the topic">
    Save the lottery settings and publish the topic; the lottery takes effect along with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/lottery-modal.png" alt="Add lottery settings window" />

### Hosting Rules

- Each topic can only have one lottery attached.
- The draw date can be at most 30 days from creation.
- The host or an admin can trigger the draw manually, or "slide to draw early".
- If the draw time arrives but there aren't enough participants, the lottery is canceled and the Energy spent on tickets is refunded.

### Editing an Ongoing Lottery

A lottery can be edited while it's open, with restrictions:

- Minimum participants can only be lowered
- Maximum participants cannot be lower than the current participant count
- Maximum tickets per user cannot be lower than the user currently holding the most tickets
- Minimum level can only be lowered
- Draw date can only be postponed
- Prize tiers can only be added; the quantity of existing prizes can only be increased

<Tip>
  As the host, you can check the participant count, tickets sold, and win rate on the lottery card at any time. Setting a reasonable minimum participant count and draw time significantly improves the chance of a successful lottery.
</Tip>
