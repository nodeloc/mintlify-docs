When you see a helpful post, besides liking it, you can also reward the author directly with Energy to show your appreciation.

## How to Reward

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the reward button">
    Click the ⚡ button in the post action bar (hovering shows "Reward this poster").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter amount and note">
    Adjust the amount in the reward popup (default 10 Energy) and optionally add a note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and send">
    Click ✓ to confirm; the Energy is transferred from your account to the author's account immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/reward-modal.png" alt="Reward input box below a post" />
</Frame>

Reward records are displayed below the post, and everyone can see who rewarded how much.

## Rules

- The reward amount must be a positive integer.
- **You cannot reward your own posts.**
- Each user can only reward a given post once.
- The per-reward limit increases with your trust level:

| Trust Level | Per-Reward Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze member (TL0) | 10 |
| Silver member (TL1) | 50 |
| Gold member (TL2) | 100 |
| Diamond member (TL3) | 200 |
| King member (TL4) | 500 |

## Withdrawing a Reward

If you change your mind after rewarding, you can click "Withdraw reward" in the post's reward records:

- The Energy is deducted back from the author's account and returned to yours.
- The author receives a "reward withdrawn" notification.

<Note>
  Rewarding is voluntary — please spend your Energy wisely. If you're short on Energy, you can build it up first through [daily check-in](/en/energy/checkin), posting, and interacting.
</Note>
