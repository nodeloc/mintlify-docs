---
title: "Topic Read Permission"
description: "Restrict topic visibility by trust level"
---

When publishing a topic, you can set a "read permission" so that only users at or above a specified trust level can view it — useful for content meant exclusively for veteran users.

## How to Set It Up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or edit a topic">
    When creating a topic, the "Read Permission" dropdown below the title defaults to "Read Permission (Everyone)". You can also adjust it when editing the first post or changing the title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the minimum level">
    Select the minimum trust level allowed to read: Bronze (TL0), Silver (TL1), Gold (TL2), Diamond (TL3), or King (TL4).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    After publishing, only users at that level or above can open the topic.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Readers See

- **Topic lists**: restricted topics show a 🔒 icon next to the title. It's gray when you have access and red when you don't.
- **Users without access** see a notice when opening the topic: "This topic requires trust level N to read."
- Restricted content is never leaked to unauthorized users through email notifications, search excerpts, RSS, or other channels.

<Note>
  Admins and moderators are not affected by read permissions and can see all topics.
</Note>

## Usage Tips

- Read permission restricts the entire topic (including all replies); it can't hide individual posts. For partial hiding, use [hidden content](/en/features/hidden-content) tags.
- High level restrictions drastically reduce readership — keep "Everyone" for regular discussions.
- For trust level rules, see [Energy and Trust Levels](/en/level).
