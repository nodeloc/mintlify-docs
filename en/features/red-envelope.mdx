---
title: "Red Envelope"
description: "Drop Energy red envelopes in topics that users can claim by replying"
---

Red envelopes are a great way to heat up a topic fast: the host deposits a sum of Energy, and users who reply to the topic can claim a random share.

## Claiming a Red Envelope

- When you see a topic with a red envelope, **simply reply to the topic to claim it automatically** — no other action needed.
- Each user can only claim once per red envelope.
- The amount you receive is random, and you'll get a notification saying "Received a red envelope of N Energy".
- Once the red envelope is fully claimed, no more shares are given out, and the top of the topic shows "The red envelope has been fully claimed!".

<Tip>
  You cannot claim your own red envelope.
</Tip>

## Sending a Red Envelope

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new topic">
    Red envelopes can only be attached to **new topics**, so write your topic content first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the red envelope button">
    Click the "Red Envelope" button at the bottom of the editor to open the creation window.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the amount and count">
    - **Total Energy**: the total amount of the red envelope, deducted from your account immediately upon creation
    - **Envelope count**: the number of shares to give out
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the topic">
    Confirm and publish the topic; the red envelope takes effect along with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules

| Rule | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Minimum total Energy | 10 |
| Minimum average Energy per share | 10 |
| Number of shares | 1 ~ 100 |
| Distribution | Random (first come, first served, while supplies last) |

- When you create a red envelope, the total Energy is **deducted from the host's account immediately**.
- Once a topic has replies, you can no longer attach a red envelope — set it up when publishing a new topic.
- The top of the topic shows "remaining envelopes" and "remaining Energy", visible to everyone.

<Note>
  If the topic is published successfully but the red envelope fails to be created (for example, due to insufficient Energy), the topic will remain but without a red envelope. Make sure your account has enough Energy before proceeding.
</Note>
