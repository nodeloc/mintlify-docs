## Create a Topic

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a node">
    Find a suitable node (section) on the homepage or in the [node list](https://www.nodeloc.com/nodes), enter it, and click "New Topic". You can also click the "New Topic" button in the top navigation directly and choose a node in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the title and content">
    Enter a clear title, choose the correct node and tags, then write the body in Markdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set read permissions (optional)">
    The "Read Permission" dropdown below the title lets you restrict the topic to users of specific trust levels — see [Topic Read Permissions](/en/features/read-permission).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click "Create Topic" to publish. Before publishing, you can also attach a [red envelope](/en/features/red-envelope) or a [lottery](/en/features/lottery).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editor Features

The editor supports Markdown syntax, and the toolbar provides common formatting: bold, italic, headings, lists, quotes, code blocks, links, image uploads, and more.

### Insert Hidden Content

The extension buttons in the toolbar let you insert three types of hidden content tags into a post:

- **Reply-to-view** `[reply]...[/reply]`: visible after the reader replies
- **Login-to-view** `[login]...[/login]`: visible to logged-in users only
- **Pay-to-view** `[pay amount=100]...[/pay]`: visible after the reader pays Energy

See [Hidden Content](/en/features/hidden-content) for detailed usage.

### Upload Images and Videos

- Click the upload button in the toolbar, or paste/drag images directly into the editor.
- Video files (mp4, mov, webm, etc., up to 500MB each) are automatically transcoded after upload and displayed in a playable inline player once transcoding completes.

## Reply and Interact

- **Reply to a topic**: click the "Reply" button at the bottom of the topic.
- **Reply to a specific post**: click the reply button in that post's action bar; it automatically quotes and links the context.
- **Like**: click ❤️ to like a post — the author earns Energy.
- **Reward**: click the ⚡ button in the post's action bar to reward the author with Energy — see [Rewards](/en/features/reward).
- **Quote**: select text in a post and click the "Quote" button that appears to quote that passage.

## Manage Your Content

- **Edit**: after posting, click the ✏️ icon in the bottom-right corner of the post to edit it within a limited time.
- **Delete**: you can delete your own posts within a short window.
- **Pin a reply**: topic authors can pin any reply in the topic to the top to highlight important information. Select "Pin" from the post's "⋯" menu; click again to unpin.

## Posting Tips

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="lightbulb" title="Choose the right node">
    Posting in the wrong node hurts visibility and your topic may be moved by an administrator. If you're unsure where to post, [browse all nodes](https://www.nodeloc.com/nodes) first to see what each node covers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="lightbulb" title="Write a good title">
    The title is key to attracting clicks. State your topic clearly and avoid uninformative titles like "Help" or "Come take a look".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion icon="lightbulb" title="Use tags wisely">
    Tags improve the chances of your post being found in search, e.g. VPS, AI, help, AFF.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
