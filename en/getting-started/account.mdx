## Create an Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the registration page">
    Visit [nodeloc.com](https://www.nodeloc.com) and click the "Sign Up" button in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your information">
    Enter your email, username, and password. The username can be changed once in Preferences after registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    You'll receive a verification email after registering — click the link in the email to complete verification. Accounts with unverified emails have limited access to some features.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use a real, accessible email address. You'll need it to recover a forgotten password, and important notifications are sent to your email.
</Tip>

## Third-Party Login

In addition to email and password, NodeLoc supports the following login methods:

| Method | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Google | One-click login with your Google account |
| GitHub | Log in with your GitHub account |
| X (Twitter) | Log in with your X account |
| Telegram | Log in with your Telegram account |
| Passkey | Log in with a device passkey such as fingerprint or face recognition |

The first time you log in with a third-party account, a linked account is created automatically. We recommend binding an email address under Preferences → Email after logging in, so you can still sign in if the third-party account has issues.

## Reset Your Password

1. Click "Forgot password" on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email and the system will send a reset link.
3. Click the link in the email to set a new password.

## Account Security Tips

- **Use a strong password**: include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols, and don't reuse passwords from other sites.
- **Add a Passkey**: add a passkey under Preferences → Security for faster, more secure logins.
- **Review login activity regularly**: you can view recent active sessions under Preferences → Security.
- **Beware of phishing**: NodeLoc's official domain is `nodeloc.com` — never enter your forum password on other websites.

## Complete Your Profile

After logging in, visit [Preferences → Profile](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/preferences/profile) to set:

- Avatar and user card background (Silver Member TL1 and above)
- Bio and signature
- Public information such as website and location
- [Telegram notifications](/en/common/telegram) linking
