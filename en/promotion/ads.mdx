## Purchase flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the promotion center">
    Click "Promote on this community" in the top navigation, or visit [/promotion](https://www.nodeloc.com/promotion).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a product">
    Click "Image ad" or "Feed ad" on a product card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter ad content">
    - **Sidebar ads**: choose a size, enter the ad name and target link, and upload ad images (up to 10).
    - **Feed ads**: enter the ad name, target link, description (≤300 characters), and multiple headlines (up to 10 — one is picked at random for each impression), and upload images (up to 10).
    
    A live preview on the right side of the form lets you switch between desktop/mobile and different views to check the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a duration and pay">
    Select the number of months, click "Proceed to payment", and complete payment with **Alipay** or **USDT (TRC20)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic activation">
    Once payment succeeds, the ad starts showing immediately and you'll receive an on-site private message notification.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Duration discounts

| Duration | Discount |
| --- | --- |
| 3 months | 20% off |
| 6 months | 40% off |
| 12 months | 50% off |

You can purchase up to 12 months at a time. Discounts apply only when purchasing exactly 3, 6, or 12 months.

## Rules and limits

- Each person may hold at most 5 ads (pending payment + active) at the same time.
- Orders must be paid within **24 hours**; unpaid orders close automatically.
- Sidebar slots are limited (10 small / 5 medium / 5 large); when exceeded, ads rotate to share impressions.
- Feed ads are inserted roughly once every 10 topics.
- Before expiry, you can renew with one click in [My promotions](https://www.nodeloc.com/promotion/my).

<Warning>
  Promotions that violate site rules will be suspended or terminated by admins, and **fees are non-refundable**. Make sure your ad content and landing pages comply.
</Warning>

## Referral program

When users who registered through your invite link purchase promotion products, you earn a **15%** cash commission on the order amount. View your earnings under Profile → Invited → Referral program. Withdrawals are available once your balance reaches ¥100 (paid out in USDT-TRC20).
