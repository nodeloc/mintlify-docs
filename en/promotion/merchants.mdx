Verified merchant is NodeLoc's official verification for merchants, helping them build credibility and gain exclusive exposure.

## Verification benefits

- Receive the **@Merchants user group** badge, with a verified merchant badge shown next to your username.
- Content posted in the [promotion node](https://www.nodeloc.com/c/business/information/10) is prioritized in the homepage recommendation area.
- Listed on the public [partners](https://www.nodeloc.com/merchants) page.

## Application process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the promotion center">
    Visit [/promotion](https://www.nodeloc.com/promotion) and click "Become a merchant".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter merchant info">
    Merchant name, website link, and merchant logo (200×200 recommended).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the annual fee">
    Verified merchant costs **¥888/year** (annual billing only, no discounts). Alipay and USDT (TRC20) are supported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic activation">
    Takes effect immediately after payment — no manual review required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notes

- Verification is valid for one year. Renew before expiry in [My promotions](https://www.nodeloc.com/promotion/my).
- If not renewed by expiry, you will be removed from the merchant group, your badge revoked, and your listing removed from the partners page.
- Merchants must still follow community rules when posting. Violations can lead to suspension or termination of the verification, with no refund.

<Note>
  Want to run ads on the site without a merchant identity? Choose [advertising](/en/promotion/ads) — monthly billing is more flexible.
</Note>
