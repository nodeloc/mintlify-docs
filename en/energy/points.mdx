Energy is NodeLoc's points system, woven through features like check-ins, rewards, red envelopes, lotteries, and paid content.

## Check Your Energy Balance and History

Visit [Profile → Energy](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/points-history) (or go directly to `/u/your-username/points-history`) to see:

- **Current total Energy**
- **Events**: details of every Energy transaction (check-in rewards, reward income, lottery ticket purchases, transfers, etc.)
- **Energy**: Energy statistics grouped by activity type

  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/points-history.png" alt="Energy history page" />
</Frame>

The Preferences → Account page also shows your current Energy balance.

## How to Earn Energy

| Action | Energy |
| --- | --- |
| Daily check-in | See [Daily Check-in](/en/energy/checkin) |
| Create a topic | +5 |
| Reply to a post | +2 |
| Receive a like | +1 |
| Receive a reply | +1 |
| Post marked as best answer | +100 |
| Invited user registers | +10 |
| Report accepted by staff | +10 |
| Visit the site daily | +1 |
| Receive a reward | The amount paid by the sender |
| Claim a red envelope | Random amount |

<Note>
  The above are the default rules. Actual values may change with operational policy — the credited amount prevails.
</Note>

## Energy Transfers

You can transfer Energy to other users.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open their user card">
    Click their avatar to open the user card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Transfer">
    Click the "Transfer" button on the card, enter an amount, and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Fees and Limits

The transfer fee is **paid by the sender on top of the transfer amount**. Rates and limits decrease/increase with trust level:

| Trust Level | Fee Rate | Min per Transfer | Max per Transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze Member (TL0) | 10% | 10 | 100 |
| Silver Member (TL1) | 5% | 5 | 500 |
| Gold Member (TL2) | 2% | 1 | 1,000 |
| Diamond Member (TL3) | 1% | 1 | 5,000 |
| King Member (TL4) | 0.5% | 1 | 10,000 |

**Example**: a Silver Member transfers 100 Energy — 105 is deducted from their balance (100 + 5 fee), and the recipient receives 100.

<Tip>
  Raising your trust level significantly reduces transfer costs. See [Energy & Trust Levels](/en/level) for level rules.
</Tip>
