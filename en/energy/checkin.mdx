Daily check-in is the easiest way to earn Energy — just log in and click once to claim it.

## How to Check In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the check-in button">
    After logging in, there's a "Daily Check-in" button in the top-right corner of the page (to the left of your avatar).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click to check in">
    Click the button — when you see "Check-in successful! You received N Energy", you're done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/checkin-modal.png" alt="Daily check-in button and confirmation message" />
</Frame>

## Rules

- **Once per day**: based on calendar days — you can only check in once per day. After checking in, the button shows "You have already checked in today".
- **Energy reward**: the check-in reward is credited directly; you can view the "Daily check-in reward" record in your [Energy history](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/points-history).
- **Reward amounts may change**: the exact amount is whatever the check-in message shows; users with higher trust levels may receive larger check-in rewards.

<Tip>
  Checking in is a good habit that adds up over time. Combine it with posting and interacting to grow your Energy faster — see [Energy System](/en/energy/points).
</Tip>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Check-in button not responding?">
    - You've already checked in today; the button can't be clicked again.
    - Check-in is rate-limited; repeated clicks in a short period are blocked — wait a moment and try again.
    - Clearing your browser cache may reset the button state, but the server still blocks duplicate check-ins, so no duplicate rewards will be credited.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Check-in Energy not credited?">
    Check your [Energy history](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/points-history) for a "Daily check-in reward" record. If the check-in appeared successful but there's no record, report it in the [Suggestions & Feedback](https://www.nodeloc.com/c/internet/suggestion/54) node.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
