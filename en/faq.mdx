<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can't I send private messages / post links / upload an avatar?">
    These features unlock as your trust level increases:
    - Initiating private messages, posting links, uploading an avatar: Silver Member (TL1)
    - Chat channels: Gold Member (TL2)
    - Creating nodes: Diamond Member (TL3)
    
    Browse and interact more to level up quickly — see [Energy & Trust Levels](/en/level).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Energy for? How do I earn it?">
    Energy can be used to reward posts, send red envelopes, buy lottery tickets, purchase paid content, transfer to others, and more. You can earn it through daily check-ins, posting, replying, receiving likes, and more — see [Energy System](/en/energy/points).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where is the check-in button? Why can't I click it?">
    After logging in, there's a "Daily Check-in" button in the top-right corner of the page; you can click it once per day. If you've already checked in, it shows "You have already checked in today" — see [Daily Check-in](/en/energy/checkin).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I join a lottery?">
    First reply to the lottery topic, then click "Join Lottery" on the lottery card to buy tickets (1 Energy each). The more tickets you buy, the higher your chance of winning, but each person can win at most once — see [Lottery](/en/features/lottery).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is the transfer fee?">
    The fee depends on your trust level, ranging from 0.5% (King) to 10% (Bronze), and is paid by the sender on top of the transfer amount. Full rates and limits: [Energy System](/en/energy/points#手续费与限额).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I edit or delete my posts?">
    Within a limited time after posting, you can click the ✏️ icon in the bottom-right corner of the post to edit it, or delete it. Topic authors can also [pin](/en/getting-started/posting#管理自己的内容) important replies.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I link Telegram notifications?">
    Contact @nler_bot on Telegram to get your Chat ID, then enter it under Preferences → Profile → Telegram Notifications. See [Telegram Notifications](/en/common/telegram) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I'm not receiving email notifications?">
    - Check your spam folder.
    - Confirm your email address and notification frequency under Preferences → Email.
    - You can use [Telegram notifications](/en/common/telegram) as an alternative.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if my account is banned or silenced?">
    Bans and silences usually result from rule violations. The page will show the reason and duration. If you disagree, you can appeal by emailing the administrator: james@nodeloc.com.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I advertise on the forum or become a verified merchant?">
    Visit the [Promotion Center](https://www.nodeloc.com/promotion) to purchase self-serve ads — Alipay and USDT are supported, and ads go live immediately after purchase. See [Advertising](/en/promotion/ads) and [Verified Merchants](/en/promotion/merchants).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add NodeLoc account login to my own website?">
    NodeLoc offers OAuth 2.0 / OIDC login integration. Gold Members (TL2) and above can create applications — see the [Developer Docs](/en/api-reference/introduction).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where can I report issues?">
    - Feature suggestions and issue reports: [Suggestions & Feedback node](https://www.nodeloc.com/c/internet/suggestion/54)
    - Documentation corrections: submit a PR to the [knowledge base repo](https://github.com/nodeloc/mintlify-docs) — see [Contribute to the Wiki](/en/create)
    - Email: james@nodeloc.com
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
