NodeLoc uses a trust level (TL) system: the more active and contributing you are, the higher your level, and the more permissions you unlock. Energy is the community's universal points currency, used for rewards, red envelopes, lotteries, and more — see [Energy System](/en/energy/points).

## Energy Earning Rules

| Action | Energy |
|------|------|
| When a user receives a like | 1 |
| When a user's post is marked as the best answer | 100 |
| When a user's invitation is redeemed | 10 |
| When a user creates a topic | 5 |
| When a user creates a post | 2 |
| When a user's report of a post is accepted by staff | 10 |
| Daily site visit | 1 |
| When a user receives a reply | 1 |

## How to Check Your Promotion Progress

The forum doesn't show a "how far until the next level" bar, but every metric behind the promotion requirements is visible on your profile:

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner and open your **profile** (or visit [/my/summary](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/summary) directly)
2. Click **Expand** to see your current **Trust Level** (e.g. "new user")
3. The **Stats** section below lists the key promotion metrics: days visited, read time, topics viewed, posts read, likes given/received, and topics/posts created

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/level-progress.png" alt="Profile page showing trust level and stats" />
</Frame>

Compare these numbers against the [requirements table](#trust-level-promotion-requirements) above to see how close you are. Once you meet the requirements, the system **promotes you automatically** — no application needed (except for TL4); promotion may take a few minutes to take effect.

<Tip>
  Some TL3 requirements only count activity from the **last 100 days**, while the stats shown here are all-time totals — the system's backend calculation is authoritative.
</Tip>

## Messaging Permissions in Detail

### Messaging Permission Tiers
1. **Receive permission** (available at TL0)  
   - Can receive personal messages and direct messages from other users  
   - Can reply to messages already received

2. **Create permission** (unlocked at TL1)  
   - Can initiate personal messages to other users  
   - Can start direct message conversations

3. **Chat permission** (unlocked at TL2)  
   - Can use real-time chat  
   - Can join group chats

---

## Key Permission Milestones

- **TL0 → TL1**  
  - Initiate private and direct messages  
  - Post links, embed media, upload an avatar  

- **TL1 → TL2**  
  - Real-time chat  
  - Custom profile background  
  - Invite users, ignore users  

- **TL2 → TL3**  
  - Create and manage tags  
  - Tag topics  

- **TL3 → TL4**  
  - Anonymous posting  
  - Shared drafts  

---

## Safety Mechanisms

- **Anti-harassment design**: TL0 users cannot initiate messages; they can only reply  
- **Gradual unlock**: more communication permissions open up as your trust level rises  
- **Chat threshold**: real-time chat requires TL2  

---

## Level Descriptions

- **TL0 (Bronze Member)**: Default starting level, no special requirements  
- **TL1 (Silver Member)**: Requires basic activity  
- **TL2 (Gold Member)**: Intermediate active user, requires a certain level of community participation  
- **TL3 (Diamond Member)**: Advanced user, requires long-term, consistent, high-quality participation; all requirements must be met within the 100-day evaluation period  
- **TL4 (King Member)**: Top-tier user; in addition to meeting all Diamond requirements, candidates must pass a community approval process — the highest recognition of community contribution
