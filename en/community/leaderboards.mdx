The sidebar provides two leaderboards showcasing the most active and the wealthiest users in the community.

## Points Leaderboard

Ranked by users' **current total Energy holdings**, reflecting wealth accumulation in the community. Visit the [Points Leaderboard](https://www.nodeloc.com/leaderboard/2) to view it, with time-range filtering supported.

## Posting Leaderboard

Ranked by **activity score** within the period — posting, replying, receiving likes, checking in, and other actions all accumulate activity points. The homepage also features the "Weekly Activity Star".

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/leaderboard-activity.png" alt="Posting Leaderboard" />
</Frame>

## How to Get Ranked

- Keep up daily [check-ins](/en/energy/checkin) and high-quality posting and interaction.
- For ways to accumulate Energy, see the [Energy system](/en/energy/points).
- Activity score rules are largely the same as the Energy earning rules — see [Energy and Trust Levels](/en/level).

<Note>
  Leaderboard data is updated daily; refer to the page's real-time display for the current rankings.
</Note>
