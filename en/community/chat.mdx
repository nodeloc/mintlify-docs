Besides asynchronous topic discussions, NodeLoc also offers real-time chat for instant conversations.

## Entering Chat

- Click the "Chat" icon in the top navigation, or visit [/chat](https://www.nodeloc.com/chat).
- Pick a channel you're interested in from the channel list and click "Join" to participate.

</Frame>

## Requirements

Real-time chat requires **Gold member (TL2)**. For how to level up, see [Energy and Trust Levels](/en/level).

## Chat Etiquette

- Chat content is subject to the same community rules, and violations are handled the same way.
- Don't spam or post ads in public channels.
- Use private messages instead of public channels for private topics.
