Community Projects (the "Friendly Recommendations" section in the sidebar) is a platform for community members to showcase their projects. You can discover interesting tools and websites here, or submit your own project for exposure and ratings.

## Browsing Projects

Visit [/projects](https://www.nodeloc.com/projects) to browse sorted by recommended, newest, highest rated, most reviewed, or most viewed. Click a project card to open its detail page with the introduction, screenshots, and community reviews.

## Submitting a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the submission form">
    Click "Submit Project" on the project list page (requires Silver member TL1 or above; up to 3 submissions per day).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in project info">
    - Project name and project URL (unique site-wide)
    - Short description (within 50 characters) and detailed description (Markdown supported)
    - Project logo (200×200 recommended)
    - Project category, tech stack, and tags
    - Repository, team size, contact info, founding date (optional)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for review">
    Check "Submit for review" to put the project into pending review — it goes public once an admin approves it. Leave it unchecked to save as a draft.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Reviewing Projects

- On the project detail page, click "Write a review" to rate it and share your experience.
- Each user can only review a project once, and can edit the review within 24 hours of posting it.
- You cannot review your own project.
- You can mark others' reviews as "helpful / not helpful", and project owners can reply to reviews.

<Tip>
  Outstanding projects may be marked as "Recommended" by admins, earning pinned exposure on the list page.
</Tip>
