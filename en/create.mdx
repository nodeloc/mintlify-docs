## Why Contribute?

The Nodeloc Wiki is dedicated to providing community members with **practical, accurate, and easy-to-understand** feature documentation and how-to guides.  
It is not the work of one person, but the collective effort of all Nodeloc users.  
Whether you're fixing documentation errors, adding usage tips, or sharing your own experience, your participation makes the Wiki better.

---

## How to Contribute

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="git-branch" title="Submit a PR via GitHub">
    1. Visit the [Nodeloc Wiki GitHub repository](https://github.com/nodeloc/mintlify-docs)
    2. Fork the repository to your account
    3. Edit or add content (Markdown/MDX format)
    4. Submit a Pull Request, **explaining the reason and content of your changes in the description**
    5. Wait for a maintainer to review and merge
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="shield-check" title="Contribution Rules">
    - Content must be truthful and reliable, with no misleading information  
    - Follow community guidelines and applicable laws  
    - Do not plagiarize copyrighted content from other platforms  
    - Keep formatting consistent with existing docs (heading hierarchy, code blocks, tables, etc.)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
