title: "Telegram Notifications"
description: "Link Telegram to receive instant forum notifications"
---

By linking Telegram, you can receive instant notifications from the NodeLoc forum, making it easy to stay on top of community activity.

## Linking steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your Chat ID">
    Open Telegram and message the bot [@nler_bot](https://t.me/nler_bot). The bot will automatically reply with your **unique Chat ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Chat ID">
    Log in to the NodeLoc forum, go to [Preferences → Profile](https://www.nodeloc.com/my/preferences/profile), find "Telegram Notifications", enter the ID returned by the bot, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start receiving notifications">
    Once saved, forum notifications such as private messages, replies, and likes will be pushed to your Telegram.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/screenshots/en/telegram-setting.png" alt="The Telegram notification field in Preferences" />
</Frame>

## Notes

- Leave the field empty to **disable** Telegram notifications.
- If you don't receive notifications, check that the ID was entered correctly, or get it again from the bot and re-save.
- Make sure Telegram message notifications are enabled and that you haven't blocked @nler_bot.

<Tip>
  Telegram notifications supplement on-site notifications. Important account security notifications are still sent by email.
</Tip>
